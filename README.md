I am **Nikhil Kumar**, Founder & CEO of **Kimi Technologies**, a technology media and development practice working across **Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Cybersecurity, and Web Solutions**. My work sits at the intersection of engineering rigour and human storytelling: shipping software that is dependable, and writing that is honest.

I build in the open. I believe source code should be readable, shared, and accountable, and that the best technology is the kind that quietly empowers people without extracting from them.

**Beyond the terminal.** I write as much as I build, and I sketch when I can. I navigate life with **Pseudobulbar Affect (PBA)**, a reminder that human emotion has its own source code, and mine simply compiles with the occasional unexpected burst of laughter or tears. It has made me a patient engineer and a kinder one.

<a id="open-source"></a>
<p align="left">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/OPEN%20SOURCE-0B0F14?style=for-the-badge&logo=opensourceinitiative&logoColor=2DD4BF" alt="Open Source" />

Open source is not a licence, it is a posture. It means showing your working, welcoming correction, and leaving the tool sharper than you found it.

- **I contribute** to projects where code meets community: developer tooling, privacy, education, and accessibility.
- **I maintain** with an emphasis on clear documentation, reproducible builds, and respectful review.
- **I believe** that shared knowledge compounds. Every good README is infrastructure for the next person.

**Want to build something?** I am open to collaborating on open-source, ethical, and affordable technology that creates real impact. Open an issue, or reach out. The door is open.
